The Art Shaala

Wear a little art.

Artshaala makes hand-painted earrings inspired by colour, craft and the everyday poetry of India. Every pair is painted by hand, so no two are exactly alike.

Colour first

Sun, sindoor and brass

Surya Kalash is painted in vermilion and turmeric sun mandalas and finished with antique brass drops. It is the boldest expression of the palette that runs through the shop: strong reds, warm yellows and metal that catches the light.

View Surya Kalash
Garden stories

Flowers drawn in gold outline

Kalamkari Bloom is a Kalamkari-inspired garden: turquoise peonies outlined in gold, painted on wood and hung with glass beads. The pattern takes its cue from India's tradition of painted and printed textiles, reimagined at earring scale.

View Kalamkari Bloom
Handmade individuality

No two pairs alike

Because each pair is painted by hand, small differences in line, dot and colour are part of the piece. The range moves from kohl black and ivory in Kohl Muse and Dhruv Phool, to white lotus on deep indigo in Sanjh Kamla, to sunflowers on periwinkle in Painted Sunshine.

Each design starts from colour and everyday imagery rather than from ornament for its own sake.
