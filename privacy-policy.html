2. What this site currently does

This site is a frontend demonstration. It has no user accounts, no server-side database and no payment processing. The checkout and contact forms do not transmit your details to Artshaala or to anyone else.

3. What is stored on your device

This site uses your browser’s local storage. These entries stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere by this site’s code:

  • artshaala-cart: the product IDs and quantities in your cart. It remains until you clear your browser data or complete a demo order.
  • artshaala-demo-order: if you complete the demo checkout, the name, email, phone, delivery address and order lines you entered, so the confirmation page can show them. You can remove it with the “Clear demo order” button on that page or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Wishlist hearts are held only in the open page and are not saved. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to read local storage.

4. Third parties
